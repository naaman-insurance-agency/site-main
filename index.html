Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? We're happy to talk through your questions.

What is life insurance?

Life insurance can provide money to your beneficiaries if you die while a qualifying policy is active. Depending on the policy, this benefit may help with housing, education, final expenses, and other financial needs.

Why might I need life insurance?

People who rely on your income or care may face financial challenges if you are no longer there. Life insurance may help support those who depend on you.

How much life insurance should I consider?

The right amount varies with your income, debts, savings, dependents, and longer-term plans. Contact us to review your situation and explore coverage options.
